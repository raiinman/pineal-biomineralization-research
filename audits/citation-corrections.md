# Citation Corrections & Drift Ledger

## A001 — Adult SCGx regression attribution

### Incorrect / drifted form
Adult established-concretion regression attributed to Welsh / Reiter juvenile denervation work.

### Correct chronology
- **Reiter, Welsh & Vaughan 1976:** bilateral SCGx at one month of age prevented later formation of concretions.
- **Champney et al. 1985:** bilateral SCGx in adult male gerbils followed by 12 weeks produced a drastic decrease in concretion number.
- **Vaughan et al. 1986:** propranolol reduced formation, supporting β-adrenergic involvement.

### Consequence
Formation prevention and established-deposit regression must remain separate claims.

## A002 — “SCGx proves chemical dissolution”

### Incorrectly strong form
SCGx causes active chemical dissolution of HAp.

### Correct status
**UNRESOLVED.**

Champney measured a major reduction in observed concretions. The study did not directly establish:
- longitudinal mineral-volume loss of individual deposits
- Ca/P mass balance
- dissolved Ca/P release
- pH-mediated demineralization
- fragment trafficking / clearance

Mechanisms remain open.

## A003 — Microglial whole-stone clearance

### Incorrectly strong form
Microglia engulf mature acervuli.

### Correct status
**NOT DEMONSTRATED.**

A narrower hypothesis remains plausible:
- acervulus destabilization / cracking
- production of mineral fragments or crystals
- microglial phagocytosis of small HAp debris

This requires direct pineal evidence.
