## Current center of gravity

The project has shifted from **“find a chemical that dissolves calcium”** toward:

> **What biological process maintains an acervulus, and what changes after sympathetic denervation that allows an established concretion population to fall?**

Leading candidate mechanisms:
1. altered pinealocyte secretory / ECM state
2. RS1-dependent mineral organization failure
3. IMPG2/HA/chondroitin-sulfate scaffold remodeling
4. altered protease balance (e.g. SERPINA1-linked ECM regulation)
5. fragmentation followed by microglial clearance of mineral debris
6. local thermodynamic changes that permit mineral loss
