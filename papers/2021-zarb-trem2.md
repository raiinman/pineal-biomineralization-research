# Zarb et al. — 2021

**Title:** Microglia control small vessel calcification via TREM2  
**Journal:** Science Advances 7(9):eabc4898  
**DOI:** 10.1126/sciadv.abc4898  
**PMID:** 33637522

## Relevance
Directly demonstrates a microglial role in controlling calcium-phosphate brain calcification outside the pineal gland.

## Project status
**Tier 3 imported mechanism.**

It supports plausibility that microglia can interact with pathological mineral, but it must not be cited as proof of pineal acervulus clearance.
