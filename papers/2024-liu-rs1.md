# Liu et al. — 2024 — RS1 / Retinoschisin

> **Repository role:** Primary-paper dossier for direct perturbation evidence that RS1 helps organize pineal mineral architecture.

**Title:** Retinoschisin Is Required for Pineal Gland Calcification and Cellular Communication in Pinealocytes of Rats and Mice  
**Journal:** Laboratory Investigation 104(7):102086  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.labinv.2024.102086  
**PMID:** 38797343

## Direct findings
- RS1 localizes in pinealocyte extracellular/membrane-associated and intracellular contexts
- RS1 perturbation changes pineal calcification architecture
- defective RS1 prevents normal binding/assembly of calcified spots/nodules
- calcareous lamellae accumulate in microvesicles in the mutant model
- RS1 colocalizes with connexin-36

## Project interpretation
RS1 is currently the strongest **specific experimentally perturbed protein candidate** for organization of pineal mineral architecture.

## Caution
Do not jump from “RS1 organizes mineral architecture in rodents” to “RS1 actively dissolves mature human acervuli.”
