# Bukreeva et al. — 2023

**Title:** Micromorphology of pineal gland calcification in age-related neurodegenerative diseases  
**Journal:** Medical Physics 50(3):1601–1613  
**DOI:** 10.1002/mp.16080  
**PMID:** 36309985

## Methods
Human postmortem pineal glands studied with micro-CT, synchrotron X-ray phase-contrast tomography, histology and immunohistochemistry.

## Key findings
Established human concrements can exhibit:
- hollow cores
- separated lamellae
- deep ragged cracks
- moderate to marked degradation

## Interpretation
Strong human evidence that calcified pineal structures can undergo structural destruction.

## Does NOT answer
- the initiating mechanism
- whether mineral chemically dissolves versus fragments
- which cells clear debris
