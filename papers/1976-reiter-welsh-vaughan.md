# Reiter, Welsh & Vaughan — 1976

**Title:** Age-related changes in the intact and sympathetically denervated gerbil pineal gland  
**Journal:** American Journal of Anatomy 146(4):427–432  
**DOI:** 10.1002/aja.1001460405

## Study role
Foundational **formation/prevention** experiment.

## Core finding
Bilateral superior cervical ganglionectomy performed at one month of age prevented the normal formation of pineal concretions and vacuoles observed later in gerbils.

## What this paper supports
- sympathetic input is involved in the normal formation of gerbil pineal concretions
- denervation changes pinealocyte morphology

## What it does NOT establish
- regression of already mature adult concretions
- chemical dissolution of HAp

## Citation-drift warning
Do not use this paper as the adult-regression citation. That role belongs to Champney et al. 1985.
