# Hartley et al. — 2015 — GSE63309

**Title:** Neurotranscriptomics: The Effects of Neonatal Stimulus Deprivation on the Rat Pineal Transcriptome  
**Journal:** PLoS One 10(9):e0137548  
**DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0137548  
**PMID:** 26367423  
**Dataset:** GSE63309

## Design
Includes control/sham, SCG removal, SCG decentralization, day/night conditions, and in-vitro norepinephrine/cAMP-responsive pineal data.

## Major result
SCG disruption largely abolishes normal night/day transcriptomic regulation while leaving much of pineal identity intact.

## High-value observation
A smaller group of genes changes in non-rhythmic abundance; the paper specifically highlights `Serpina1`, a secreted protease inhibitor potentially relevant to extracellular architecture.

## Project use
Reanalyze candidate genes/pathways:
- Impg2
- Rs1
- Serpina1
- Mmp3
- Spp1
- Slc20a2 / Xpr1
- Enpp1 / Ankh / Alpl
- HA / chondroitin-sulfate metabolism
- lysosomal / phagocytic genes

## Caveat
The in-vivo surgical manipulation was neonatal, not an acute adult Champney-style intervention.
