# P0006 — Ibañez Rodriguez et al. — 2018

> **Repository role:** Primary-paper dossier for the acute microglial and Wallerian-degeneration response following adult SCGx.

**Research ID:** P0006  
**Title:** Differential Response of Pineal Microglia to Surgical versus Pharmacological Stimuli  
**Journal:** Journal of Comparative Neurology 526(15):2462–2481  
**DOI:** 10.1002/cne.24505  
**PMID:** 30246867  
**PMCID:** PMC6196128

## Direct pineal findings

Adult rat bilateral SCGx:
- caused Wallerian degeneration of sympathetic fibers within the pineal gland;
- significantly increased Iba1+ microglial density by day 3;
- the increased microglial density persisted through day 9 and returned toward sham baseline by day 13;
- clustered Iba1+/PCNA+/ED1+ activated/proliferative microglia were increased around day 4 after surgical disruption;
- the percentage of Pax6+ pineal cells contacted and/or phagocytosed by microglia increased significantly after SCGx.

## Why this matters

Champney's adult-regression endpoint was 12 weeks after SCGx. P0006 independently identifies an **early 3–13 day pineal remodeling/clearance response** after sympathetic denervation, making it one of the strongest directly relevant time-window papers for `RQ001`.

## What this paper does NOT establish

- that microglia phagocytose pineal acervuli;
- that microglia contain calcium/phosphate or hydroxyapatite after SCGx;
- that the microglial response causes Champney's later concretion loss;
- that mature mineral chemically dissolves.

## Current project use

P0006 supports the existence of an early post-SCGx cellular response window. It does **not** close the mechanistic gap between denervation and later loss of established concretions.

Linked questions:
- `RQ001` — reconstruct days 0–14 after adult SCGx;
- `RQ006` — find pineal microglia containing Ca/P or HAp material.
