# P0006 — Ibañez Rodriguez et al. — 2018

> **Repository role:** Primary-paper dossier for the acute microglial and Wallerian-degeneration response following adult SCGx.

**Research ID:** P0006  
**Title:** Differential Response of Pineal Microglia to Surgical versus Pharmacological Stimuli  
**PMID:** 30246867  
**PMCID:** PMC6196128

## Direct pineal findings
Adult rat SCGx:
- produces Wallerian degeneration of sympathetic fibers
- increases Iba1+ pineal microglial density by ~day 3
- increases clustered Iba1+/PCNA+/ED1+ activated/proliferative cells
- altered microglial contact/phagocytosis involving Pax6+ pineal cells
- response declines toward sham baseline by day 13

## Why this matters
Champney's final endpoint was 12 weeks. This paper identifies an early **3–13 day biological clearance/remodeling phase** following the same class of intervention.

## Unresolved
Whether these microglia interact with acervular mineral or HAp fragments.
