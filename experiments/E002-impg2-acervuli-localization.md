# E002 — IMPG2/SPACRCAN Localization in Human Acervuli

> **Repository role:** Defines the direct spatial experiment that can confirm or substantially weaken IMPG2/SPACRCAN as a human acervulus scaffold candidate.

## Question
Is IMPG2/SPACRCAN physically associated with human pineal acervuli?

## Proposed spatial panel
- IMPG2 / SPACRCAN
- hyaluronan
- chondroitin sulfate
- calcium / phosphate mineral
- GFAP
- RS1 where technically feasible

## Spatial regions
- core
- inner lamellae
- outer lamellae
- immediate boundary
- distant pineal parenchyma

## Interpretation
- lamellar enrichment → strong scaffold evidence
- boundary-only → possible organizer/barrier role
- no association → weakens direct scaffold hypothesis
