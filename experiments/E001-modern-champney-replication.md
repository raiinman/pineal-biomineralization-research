# E001 — Modern Champney Replication

> **Repository role:** Defines the decisive modern experiment for determining whether individual pre-existing adult concretions truly lose mineral after SCGx.

## Question
Do individual pre-existing adult pineal concretions lose mineral after bilateral SCGx?

## Conceptual design
- adult calcification-bearing animal model
- baseline non-destructive high-resolution mineral imaging
- bilateral SCGx vs sham
- serial imaging across early and late time points
- terminal histology / elemental analysis

## Core readouts
- concretion count
- individual mineral volume
- mineral density
- fragmentation
- Ca/P spatial distribution
- tissue location of mineral debris
- microglial association
- RS1 / IMPG2 / ECM markers

## Critical value
Separates:
- true mineral loss
- fragmentation
- relocation
- counting artifact
- failure of maintenance
