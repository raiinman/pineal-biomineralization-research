# Research Templates

> **Repository role:** Standardizes how future claims and primary-paper reconstructions are recorded so evidence boundaries remain consistent.

## Templates

- `CLAIM_TEMPLATE.md` — structure for atomic scientific claims, evidence, replication, and falsification.
- `PAPER_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md` — structure for primary-paper reconstruction, direct observations, interpretation, and citation drift.

Use these templates whenever a new research lane is promoted into the canonical archive.
