# Pathway — Microglial Clearance

> **Repository role:** Mechanistic pathway file separating direct pineal microglial evidence from imported evidence about calcium-phosphate clearance elsewhere in the brain.

## What is directly established in pineal tissue
SCGx causes a transient microglial activation/proliferation response in adult rat pineal glands.

## What is established outside pineal tissue
Microglia can control brain calcium-phosphate calcification and can phagocytose hydroxyapatite-related material.

## Working hypothesis
Mature acervulus  
→ ECM/mineral destabilization  
→ cracks / fragment generation  
→ exposed small HAp particles  
→ microglial phagocytosis / clearance

## Evidence rating
**Tier 5 mechanistic inference.**

## Do not claim
- microglia swallow whole acervuli
- microglia are proven to drive Champney regression

## Decisive experiment
Co-localize:
- Iba1 / TMEM119 / CD68
- calcium / phosphate / HAp
- acervulus boundary and fragments
before and after adult SCGx.
