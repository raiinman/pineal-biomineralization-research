# Pathway — RS1 / Retinoschisin

## Current status
Top specific experimentally supported molecular candidate for **pineal mineral architecture**.

## Evidence
Liu et al. 2024 demonstrates that disrupting RS1 changes where and how pineal mineral structures assemble.

## Working role
RS1 may act as an organizer of:
- pinealocyte cell-cell / cell-matrix organization
- mineral-containing microvesicles
- extracellular calcified spot/nodule architecture

## Unknowns
- adult human acervulus localization at lamellar resolution
- response to SCGx / norepinephrine
- role in maintaining an already mature concretion
- whether loss of RS1 promotes fragmentation or only abnormal formation
