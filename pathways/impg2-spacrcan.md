# Pathway — IMPG2 / SPACRCAN

> **Repository role:** Mechanistic pathway file for the unresolved proteoglycan/HA scaffold hypothesis.

## Current status
High-value candidate, but direct acervulus evidence remains absent.

## Why it remains compelling
- made by pinealocytes
- chondroitin proteoglycan
- highly glycosylated
- hyaluronan binding
- strong ECM-organizing chemistry in retina

## Key falsifiable prediction
If IMPG2 is part of the human acervulus scaffold, staining/proteomics should show spatial enrichment:
- within organic-rich lamellae
- at mineral/organic interfaces
- at the acervulus boundary
or a reproducible surrounding pattern.

A clean negative result would substantially weaken the hypothesis.
