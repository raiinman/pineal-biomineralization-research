# Mechanistic Pathways

> **Repository role:** Organizes evidence by biological mechanism rather than by paper, allowing cross-study causal chains to be tested and updated.

## Current pathways

- `sympathetic-signaling.md` — SCG/norepinephrine control of pineal physiology and calcification phenotypes.
- `rs1-retinoschisin.md` — RS1 as a candidate organizer of mineral architecture.
- `impg2-spacrcan.md` — IMPG2/SPACRCAN extracellular-matrix scaffold hypothesis.
- `microglia-clearance.md` — evidence and limits of mineral-fragment clearance by microglia.

## Standard

Every pathway must distinguish direct pineal evidence from mechanisms imported from other tissues or disease models.
