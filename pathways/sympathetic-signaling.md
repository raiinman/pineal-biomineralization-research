# Pathway — Sympathetic / Adrenergic Control

> **Repository role:** Mechanistic pathway file linking SCG/norepinephrine signaling to pineal physiology, concretion formation, and the unresolved adult-regression phenotype.

## Established chain

SCN/light-dark control  
→ superior cervical ganglion  
→ norepinephrine release in pineal  
→ β-adrenergic / cAMP and α-adrenergic signaling  
→ broad pinealocyte transcriptional, metabolic, secretory and structural effects

## Calcification evidence

- Juvenile SCGx prevents normal concretion formation — Reiter 1976.
- Adult SCGx drastically reduces established concretion number after 12 weeks — Champney 1985.
- Propranolol suppresses concretion formation — Vaughan 1986.
- SCGx alters transcriptomic programs — Hartley 2015.
- SCGx triggers acute microglial remodeling — Ibañez Rodriguez 2018.

## Central mechanistic gap

**What specific SCG-dependent maintenance process stabilizes an established acervulus?**

- ECM secretion / protease control
- RS1
- IMPG2 / HA / chondroitin sulfate
