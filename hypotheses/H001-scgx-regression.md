# H001 — Adult SCGx causes active regression of established pineal concretions

## Observation
Champney et al. 1985 reported drastic reduction in adult gerbil pineal concretion number 12 weeks after bilateral SCGx.

## Hypothesis
The intervention shifts the mature acervulus from a maintenance/stability state into net loss through one or more of:
1. scaffold destabilization
2. mineral dissolution
3. fragmentation
4. debris clearance
5. altered tissue transport

## Current confidence
**Phenotype: MODERATE–HIGH (single key experiment).**  
**Mechanism: LOW / UNRESOLVED.**

## Falsification
A modern longitudinal replication showing no loss of pre-existing mineral volume would substantially weaken the hypothesis.
