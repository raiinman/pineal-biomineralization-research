# Scientific Timeline

> **Repository role:** Chronological map of the experiments that changed the formation, maintenance, and regression models.

## 1976 — Prevention
**Reiter RJ, Welsh MG, Vaughan MK.**  
*Age-related changes in the intact and sympathetically denervated gerbil pineal gland.*  
American Journal of Anatomy 146(4):427–432.  
DOI: 10.1002/aja.1001460405

- One-month-old gerbils received bilateral SCGx.
- Normal age-related formation of concretions and vacuoles was prevented.
- **Interpretation:** sympathetic input is involved in formation.
- **Do not miscite this as the adult regression experiment.**

## 1985 — Adult regression phenotype
**Champney TH, Joshi BN, Vaughan MK, Reiter RJ.**  
*Superior cervical ganglionectomy results in the loss of pineal concretions in the adult male gerbil (Meriones unguiculatus).*  
The Anatomical Record 211(4):465–468.  
DOI: 10.1002/ar.1092110414

- Adult male gerbils.
- Bilateral SCGx.
- Twelve-week endpoint.
- Concretion number drastically decreased irrespective of photoperiod treatment.
- **Central unresolved question:** what physically happened to the pre-existing mineral?

## 1986 — Pharmacological formation evidence
**Vaughan MK, Joshi BN, Reiter RJ.**  
*Daily propranolol administration reduces pineal concretion formation in the Mongolian gerbil.*  
DOI: 10.3181/00379727-182-42354

- β-adrenergic blockade reduced formation of vacuoles and concretions.
- Supports sympathetic/adrenergic control of formation.
- Not a direct adult-regression replication.

## 2000 — IMPG2 / SPACRCAN candidate
**Acharya S et al.**  
*SPACRCAN, a novel human interphotoreceptor matrix hyaluronan-binding proteoglycan synthesized by photoreceptors and pinealocytes.*  
J Biol Chem. DOI: 10.1074/jbc.275.10.6945

- Pinealocyte expression established.
- Chondroitin proteoglycan; hyaluronan binding.
- Direct presence inside human acervuli still not demonstrated.

## 2015 — Neurotranscriptomic bridge
**Hartley SW et al.**  
*Neurotranscriptomics: The Effects of Neonatal Stimulus Deprivation on the Rat Pineal Transcriptome.*  
PLoS One 10(9):e0137548. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0137548.  
Dataset: GSE63309

- SCGx / decentralization removes most night/day transcriptional rhythms.
- Dataset also contains norepinephrine and cAMP treatment arms.
- High-value dataset for ECM/mineral candidate reanalysis.

## 2018 — Pineal microglial response to SCGx
**Ibañez Rodriguez MP et al.**  
*Differential Response of Pineal Microglia to Surgical versus Pharmacological Stimuli.*  
J Comp Neurol. PMID: 30246867

- SCGx causes Wallerian degeneration.
- Iba1+ microglia increase by day 3.
- Activated/proliferating ED1/CD68-associated microglial phenotype rises.
- Returns toward baseline by ~day 13.

## 2021 — Brain calcification clearance analogy
**Zarb Y et al.**  
*Microglia control small vessel calcification via TREM2.*  
Science Advances 7(9):eabc4898. DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.abc4898

- Establishes microglial control of calcium-phosphate calcification outside pineal tissue.
- Mechanistically relevant but imported evidence.

## 2023 — Human destruction morphology
**Bukreeva I et al.**  
*Micromorphology of pineal gland calcification in age-related neurodegenerative diseases.*  
Medical Physics 50(3):1601–1613. DOI: 10.1002/mp.16080

- Human postmortem micro-CT / XPCT.
- Hollow cores, separated layers, deep ragged cracks and degradation reported.
- Supports dynamic/destructive behavior of established concrements.

## 2024 — RS1 architecture
**Liu X et al.**  
*Retinoschisin Is Required for Pineal Gland Calcification and Cellular Communication in Pinealocytes of Rats and Mice.*  
Laboratory Investigation 104(7):102086. DOI: 10.1016/j.labinv.2024.102086

- Direct perturbation of a specific secreted pineal protein changes mineral architecture.
- RS1 is now a top specific molecular candidate for mineral organization.
