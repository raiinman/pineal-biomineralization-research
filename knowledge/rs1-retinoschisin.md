# RS1 / Retinoschisin

## Evidence anchor
`P0009` / `C008`: perturbing RS1 changes pineal calcification architecture in rodents.

## Current interpretation
RS1 is currently the strongest specific experimentally perturbed protein candidate for organizing pineal mineral architecture.

## What is not established
- direct causal role in mature human acervulus maintenance
- SCG/NE regulation of RS1
- RS1-driven dissolution or clearance
