# IMPG2 / SPACRCAN

## Evidence anchor
`P0004` / `C006`: IMPG2/SPACRCAN is produced by pinealocytes and is a large HA-binding chondroitin proteoglycan.

## Current interpretation
IMPG2 is a strong biochemical candidate for a pinealocyte-derived extracellular scaffold, but direct incorporation into human acervuli has not been demonstrated.

## Critical boundary
`C007` remains unresolved: pinealocyte expression is not acervulus localization.
