# Microglial Clearance

## Pineal evidence
`P0006` / `C010`: adult SCGx triggers a transient increase in activated/proliferating pineal microglia during the early post-denervation period.

## Imported mechanism
`P0007` / `C011`: outside pineal tissue, microglia can control and phagocytose calcium-phosphate / HAp-related calcification.

## Current hypothesis
`H004`: pineal microglia may clear small mineral fragments or debris after acervulus destabilization.

## Boundary
The project does **not** claim that microglia engulf whole mature acervuli.
