# Sympathetic Control

## Established chain
SCN/light-dark signaling → superior cervical ganglion → norepinephrine → pinealocyte adrenergic signaling.

## Calcification evidence
- `P0001` / `C002` — juvenile SCGx prevents normal concretion formation.
- `P0002` / `C003` — adult SCGx is followed by drastic reduction of established concretions.
- `P0003` / `C004` — propranolol reduces formation.
- `P0005` / `C009` — SCG manipulation strongly alters pineal transcriptional rhythms.
- `P0006` / `C010` — SCGx triggers an early pineal microglial response.
