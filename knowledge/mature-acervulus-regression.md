# Mature Acervulus Regression

## Central question
What can make an already established pineal acervulus lose mineral mass, fragment, or disappear?

## Strongest anchors
- `P0002` / `C003` — Champney 1985 adult SCGx regression phenotype.
- `P0008` / `C013` — human concrements can show cracks, hollowing, separated lamellae and structural degradation.

## Current model
A mature acervulus may require continuing biological maintenance. A state change could alter the mineral-organic interface, local ion thermodynamics, or structural integrity, followed by dissolution and/or fragmentation and debris clearance.

## Boundary
`C005`: Champney does not by itself prove chemical HAp dissolution.
