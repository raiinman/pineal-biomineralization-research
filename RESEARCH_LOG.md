# Research Log

## 2026-08-20 — Canonical repository bootstrap

### Problem
Research had become distributed across long conversation windows and project files, creating risk of citation drift and loss of intermediate reasoning.

### Repository decision
GitHub will be the canonical research ledger. Zotero is recommended later as the paper/PDF reference library.

### Major corrections preserved
- Reiter 1976 = **juvenile prevention**, not adult regression.
- Champney 1985 = **adult established-concretion loss phenotype**.
- “SCGx chemically dissolves HAp” is **unresolved**, not demonstrated and not properly labeled contradicted.
- Exact adult Champney replication has not yet been located.

### Major mechanistic upgrades
- RS1 moved to the top tier of **specific experimental pineal mineral-architecture candidates**.
- IMPG2/SPACRCAN remains a high-value scaffold candidate but still lacks direct human-acervulus localization.
- SCGx causes an acute pineal microglial response, creating a plausible clearance phase.
- General brain literature establishes that microglia can interact with / clear calcium-phosphate mineral, but this has not been directly demonstrated for pineal acervuli.
- GSE63309 is a high-value existing transcriptomic dataset linking SCG manipulation with norepinephrine/cAMP response.

### Current central question
**What molecular event during the first 3–14 days after adult SCGx converts a stable mature acervulus into something that is drastically reduced 12 weeks later?**
